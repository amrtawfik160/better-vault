title: "Accounts and subscriptions"
description: "Connect an AI subscription you already pay for, such as ChatGPT or Claude, or sign in to an optional deepLe profile. Neither is needed to use the app."
---

deepLe works fully without an account: connect an AI subscription you already pay for, add an API key, or use a local model.

## Connect an AI subscription

Go to **Settings → AI → AI subscriptions**, then choose ChatGPT, Claude, or another supported provider. The provider opens its sign-in page and the app loads your models when you return. [Providers and models](/docs/providers#connect-an-ai-subscription) lists the supported plans and features.

## Sign in to your profile

Open **Settings → Account and plan** to sign in or create an optional profile with a username. AI subscription sign-in happens in **AI subscriptions**, not here.

## Privacy

Your notebooks, notes and API keys stay on your device and never go to an account. The account itself is your email, your name and your username.
