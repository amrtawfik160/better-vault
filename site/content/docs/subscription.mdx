## Connect an AI subscription

Go to **Settings → AI → AI subscriptions**, then choose ChatGPT, Claude, or another supported provider. The provider opens its sign-in page and the app loads your models when you return. See [Providers](/docs/providers#connect-an-ai-subscription) for supported features.

## Sign in to your profile

Open **Settings → Account and plan** to sign in or create an optional profile. AI subscription sign-in happens in **AI subscriptions**.

## Manage an existing Better Vault plan

If you have an active Better Vault plan, sign in to your Better Vault account and open **Settings → Account and plan → Manage existing plan**. Polar's customer portal handles invoices and cancellation.
