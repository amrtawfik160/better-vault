A notebook is a workspace for one topic, course or project, with its own sources, chats and Studio outputs.

## The notebook screen

A notebook opens on its overview. It shows where you left off in the document you read last, with **Continue reading**, how many of its cards are due, and everything in it: its documents and the outputs made from them, each one click from being read or opened.

Beside the overview sits the **companion**, with three tabs. **Ask** is the notebook's chat, with **History** and **New question** at its top. **Practise** starts an exercise on the notebook, and **Make** turns it into cards or [Studio](/docs/studio) outputs. On a wide screen the companion stays docked; on a narrower one it opens over the page and Escape closes it.

The header holds **Add**, **Practise this** and the button that shows or hides the companion. The notebook's name opens a menu to switch notebooks or to create, rename or delete one.

## Create a notebook

Choose **New notebook** on the notebooks page, which is the app's home, or from the name menu. Your next step is to add [sources](/docs/sources).

## Chats and History

A notebook can hold several chats. Open **History** at the top of Ask to see them grouped by day. From there you can open an older chat, rename or delete it, or start a **New chat**.

## Delete a notebook

Open the name menu and choose **Delete notebook**. This removes its sources, chats and outputs for good, so export a backup in **Settings → Storage and data** first if you may want them back.
