---
title: "Privacy and your data"
description: "better-vault is local-first. Notebooks, sources, chats and keys stay in your browser or in a file on your computer, and nothing is ever kept on a server."
---

better-vault keeps everything on your device, and nothing is stored on a server.

In the web app, notebooks, sources, chats, Studio outputs and API keys live in your browser's IndexedDB. In the [desktop app](/docs/desktop) they live in one SQLite file in the app's data folder on your computer. Either way there is no account and no server database.

When you [connect an AI subscription](/docs/providers#connect-an-ai-subscription), the local proxy keeps sign-in tokens in its private directory on your computer. Provider tokens stay out of the renderer and notebook backups. The selected provider receives your prompts and any passages included for the answer. Use **Disconnect** to remove the local account credential. The original desktop ChatGPT connection stores its own tokens in the desktop data folder.

## What leaves your device?

Only what a request needs. When you chat or create in the Studio, better-vault sends the relevant passages from your selected sources, your question and your key to its API route. The route forwards them to the provider you picked and streams the answer back, without storing or logging them.

When you add a PDF or a web page, the file or address goes to the parse route, which returns plain text and keeps nothing. For a YouTube video, that route asks YouTube for the video's captions; nothing about you is sent. If you have Gemini transcribe a video without captions, its address and your Google key go to Google for that one request. When the model list loads, your key is used to ask the provider which models you can use.

Reading aloud stays on your device. English is read by Kokoro, an open voice model that runs inside the app. The first time you press **Listen**, the app downloads the model from Hugging Face (about 330 MB with WebGPU, 92 MB without) and keeps it for next time; only the model is downloaded, and your text is never sent. Until it is ready, your system voice reads. **Settings → Reading and voice** shows the download, and lets you start, cancel or remove it. Other languages use your system's own voices.

With Ollama or LM Studio, all of these requests stay on your own machine. In the desktop app, the API routes also run on your computer.

## Backups

Open **Settings → Storage and data** to export all your notebooks as a JSON file, or to import one. Backups never contain your API keys. Older backups stay importable when the storage format changes, and a backup from the web app imports into the desktop app.

Clearing your browser's site data deletes the web app's notebooks, so export a backup first.

## Hosting for others

If you host better-vault for other people, their notebooks and keys stay in their own browsers and never reach you. [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting) lists the settings that harden a public deployment.
